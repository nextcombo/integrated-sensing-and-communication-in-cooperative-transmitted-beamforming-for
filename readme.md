Article Illustrations
Finally, opinions and future work on the integration of sensors and communication are expected to revolutionize wireless networks by seamlessly integrating sensor functions into communication systems. This integration will enable next-generation networks (such as 6G) to support a wide range of applications, including autonomous vehicles, smart cities, and advanced Internet of Things solutions. The integration of this technology with cellular architecture is an exciting prospect for the future of mobile communications. This paper focuses on the downlink direction of an ISAC system with cooperative beamforming.
The main goal of this paper is to achieve a CF ISAC system with cooperative beamforming, with a particular emphasis on angle detection. There are several aspects related to this topic that could lead to further research development. Therefore, the following are two main examples that can be studied in future work to achieve a more complete radar:
·Further development of the system to consider target distance and relative speed;
·Evaluation of other algorithms for angle estimation, such as Multi-Signal Classification (MUSIC) and Estimation of Signal Parameters via Rotation Invariant Techniques (ESPRIT).
